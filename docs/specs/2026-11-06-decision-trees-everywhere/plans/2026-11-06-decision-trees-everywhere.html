Risks and when the plan stops

  • S5's first step confirms that lint runs on a temporary copy of the repository. If it can't, S5 stops and the case-reading design comes back to you; no workaround is built.
  • A rule may not meet U17's case floors. Then it stays off, and you see what it lacks. No made-up cases are added.
  • The held-out run may show a false warning or a missed real mistake. Then that rule stays off and you see the results. The next try needs fresh held-out cases, and there is no rewording against the same cases.
  • Any change to a public rulebook field beyond this design stops the work and comes back as a design change.
  • Removing the TS gate touches tests that check it. Each one gets keep, repair or remove with its reason, in PR 2.
