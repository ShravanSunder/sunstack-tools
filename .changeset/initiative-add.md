`demistify initiative add <choice|ruling|assumption> --namespace <namespace> --page <page.html>` scaffolds a record with `TODO:` placeholders, its section item on the page, and the owner's verbatim words with `--words`, then refreshes the generated views (restoring every changed file when the check fails). It previews by default; `--write` applies.
