One family of content containers

The corner decision is settled: 8 px for ordinary content containers. Compare three treatments of the same card side by side, with one base palette and identical content.

The chosen convention

Use the shared 8 px radius for ordinary content containers, including cards, panels, tables and selected-item details. Keep 4 px for small inset elements and compact controls, circles for round controls, and diagram shapes where they carry meaning. Any other container radius needs a specific reason. This records the chosen convention; production styles are unchanged.

The square selected-item source panel in the supplied Explorer view does not consistently use the radius already present on nearby Explorer surfaces. The first production repair would belong to that container's shared style.
