Color that keeps its meaning

Color should answer “what kind of thing is this?” consistently across views. Here Boundary, Store and State each have an explicit palette slot. Selecting an item changes its outline, not its category.

Keep category, status and selection independent

Mermaid currently assigns its six palette slots by encountered identity order. It does not infer domain types. Explorer's current kinds have another mapping. A shared convention needs a declared category mapping, visible labels and stable identity—not a promise that any colored box means success.

The chosen card treatment is a subtle tint. A diagram can use stronger fills from the same palette; card titles and icons stay neutral, while selection and status remain independent.
