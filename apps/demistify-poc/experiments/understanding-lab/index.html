What is real here

The components and interactions are real local prototypes. The requirements, source locations and verification results are illustrative fixtures. A green example is not evidence that a product requirement is satisfied.

The owner pages use ordinary HTML. The retained call-stack reader and shared navigation, inspection and Comments come from the local library build. They share fonts, palette and spacing; no framework, backend or custom theme is required.
