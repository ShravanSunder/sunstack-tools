What is real here

The components and interactions are real local prototypes. The requirements, source locations and verification results are illustrative fixtures. A green example is not evidence that a product requirement is satisfied.

Both active experiments use the local library build, shared fonts, palette and spacing. No framework, backend or custom theme is required. The proposed components live in this demonstration app until we choose which ideas deserve a library contract.
