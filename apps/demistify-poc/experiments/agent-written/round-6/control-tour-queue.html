# Delivery queue deep dive The delivery queue holds messages until a channel worker sends them, and retries failures. Back to the [notification service overview](control-tour-overview.html). ## Message states A message goes from queued to sending, then to sent. A temporary failure moves it to retrying, and a retry returns it to sending. Permanent failures, such as an invalid address or an unsubscribed user, go straight to dead. After the last attempt, a retrying message goes to dead. ## Retry policy Backoff is exponential, starting at 10 seconds and doubling, for at most 6 attempts, with up to 20% jitter. ## Configuration | Setting | Value | | --- | --- | | `QUEUE_MAX_ATTEMPTS` | 6 | | `QUEUE_BASE_DELAY_MS` | 10000 | | `QUEUE_MAX_DELAY_MS` | 900000 (new) | | `QUEUE_WORKERS_PER_CHANNEL` | 8 | ## The retry change (#341) Caps the backoff in `src/queue/retry.ts` and adds the cap as a new setting in `src/queue/policy.ts`. The capped line is the fix: the backoff is capped before jitter is added. Jitter is added after the cap, so a wait can pass 15 minutes where the cap binds. The new setting, 15 × 60,000 ms, is the same value as QUEUE_MAX_DELAY_MS (900000). Not at these settings. The base delay doubles from 10 s, so attempt 6 is 10 min 40 s before jitter, already under the 15-minute cap. The cap first binds at attempt 7 (21 min 20 s), beyond the six-attempt policy. ## Known issues Dead messages are kept for 14 days, but nothing alerts on a spike in dead messages. Push retries can deliver duplicates when the push service times out after it has accepted a message.
