# Commerce domain map One place to see every entity in the commerce domain, where it lives, and how entities connect. Illustrative domain, dated 2026-10-14. Five areas own twenty entities. An order is the spine: identity places it, catalog prices it, billing pays for it, fulfilment delivers it. An order is the spine. Identity places it, catalog prices it, billing pays for it, fulfilment delivers it. A payment is an attempt to collect money. It issues the invoice and can be reversed by a refund. A shipment is goods leaving one warehouse for one order. ## Map Each entity appears once in the diagram, grouped by area. Clicking a node that matches a card opens that card. ## Areas and entities Each area is a region. Open one to read its entities. Fields and rules sit under each card's details. Who the customer is. Person or company that places orders.
Fields and rule Fields: id, name, email, type (person or company), created at. Rule: email is unique.
Sign-in credentials for a customer.
Fields and rule Fields: customer id, password hash, MFA enabled, last sign-in. Rule: one account per customer.
Postal address, used for shipping and invoices.
Fields Fields: lines, city, postcode, country.
Marketing and data-processing consents.
Fields and rule Fields: customer id, purpose, granted at, version. Rule: append-only; latest version wins.
What can be sold. A sellable item family.
Fields Fields: id, name, description, status (draft, live or retired).
A specific size or colour.
Fields Fields: sku, product id, attributes, weight.
Price of a SKU in a currency.
Fields and rule Fields: sku, currency, amount, valid from. Rule: one active price per SKU and currency.
Stock per SKU per warehouse.
Fields Fields: sku, warehouse id, on hand, reserved.
Carts become orders. Items before checkout.
Fields and rule Fields: id, customer id (optional), lines, expires at. Rule: expires after 7 days idle.
The committed purchase.
Fields and states Fields: id, customer id, status, totals, placed at. States: placed, then paid, then fulfilled, then closed. Cancelled from placed or paid.
One SKU in an order.
Fields and rule Fields: sku, quantity, unit price at purchase. Rule: price is copied, never re-read.
A promotion applied to an order.
Fields and rule Fields: code, amount or percent. Rule: at most one per order.
Money in and out. An attempt to collect money.
Fields Fields: id, order id, method, amount, status (authorised, captured, failed or refunded).
Legal document issued when an order is paid.
Fields and rule Fields: number, order id, billing address, tax lines. Rule: numbers are sequential per country.
Money returned.
Fields and rule Fields: payment id, amount, reason. Rule: total refunds are at most the captured amount.
Rate by country and product class.
Fields Fields: country, class, percent.
Getting it to the customer. A stocking location.
Fields Fields: id, address, cut-off time.
Goods leaving one warehouse for one order.
Fields Fields: id, order id, warehouse id, status (pending, packed, shipped or delivered).
A physical box in a shipment.
Fields Fields: tracking number, carrier, weight.
Goods coming back.
Fields and effect Fields: order line, reason, received at. Triggers a refund.

Changing a Price never changes an existing order. Inventory is reserved at checkout and released if payment fails within 15 minutes. A cancelled order that was already paid needs a Refund. Cancelling does not refund by itself. ## Open questions Should Address become an entity with an id so invoices can reference it after edits? Do company customers need multiple accounts?
