# Delivery queue deep dive Illustrative service, dated 2026-10-14. Deep dive from the [service overview](treatment-tour-overview.html). The queue holds messages until a channel worker sends them, and retries failures. Messages move through queued, sending, retrying, sent and dead. Retries use exponential backoff with at most 6 attempts. Change #341 caps the backoff at 15 minutes. ## Message states A message is queued, then sending. A temporary failure sends it to retrying and back to sending. A permanent failure goes straight to dead. ## Retry policy Permanent failures go straight to dead. - QUEUE_MAX_ATTEMPTS: 6 - QUEUE_BASE_DELAY_MS: 10000 - added: QUEUE_MAX_DELAY_MS: 900000 (new) - QUEUE_WORKERS_PER_CHANNEL: 8 ## Retry change #341 Caps the base delay at 15 minutes and moves the policy into its own file, `src/queue/policy.ts`. The cap is applied before jitter, so jitter of up to 20% is still added on top of the capped value. Removed: the uncapped base plus jitter. Attempt 6 used to wait over 10 minutes. The fix: the base delay is capped at policy.maxDelayMs before jitter is added. Jitter is still added, on top of the capped value. New setting: 15 × 60,000 ms, which is 900,000 ms. At attempt 6 the base delay is 10 s × 2^6 = 640 s, about 10.7 minutes. That is under the 900 s cap. Confirm which attempts the cap changes before relying on it. ## Known issues Dead messages are kept 14 days. Nothing alerts on a spike in dead messages. A push retry can deliver a duplicate when the push service times out after it has accepted a message. Back to the [service overview](treatment-tour-overview.html).
