# Commerce domain at a glance Illustrative domain, dated 2026-10-14. For an engineer joining the team: every entity, where it lives, and how entities connect. Five areas own twenty entities. An order is the spine: identity places it, catalog prices it, billing pays for it, fulfilment delivers it. Start with Order, the committed purchase that the other areas hang off. An order line copies its price at purchase. Changing a Price never changes an existing order. A cancelled order that was already paid needs a Refund. Cancelling does not refund by itself. A Shipment leaves one Warehouse for one order. ## Map Who the customer is. *Aggregate.* Person or company that places orders. Fields: id, name, email, type (person | company), created at. **Rule:** email is unique. *Entity.* Sign-in credentials for a customer. Fields: customer id, password hash, MFA enabled, last sign-in. **Rule:** one account per customer. *Value.* Postal address. Fields: lines, city, postcode, country. Used for shipping and invoices. *Entity.* Marketing and data-processing consents. Fields: customer id, purpose, granted at, version. **Rule:** append-only; latest version wins. What can be sold. *Aggregate.* A sellable item family. Fields: id, name, description, status (draft | live | retired). *Entity.* A specific size or colour. Fields: sku, product id, attributes, weight. *Value.* Price of a SKU in a currency. Fields: sku, currency, amount, valid from. **Rule:** one active price per SKU and currency. *Entity.* Stock per SKU per warehouse. Fields: sku, warehouse id, on hand, reserved. Carts become orders. *Aggregate.* Items before checkout. Fields: id, customer id (optional), lines, expires at. **Rule:** expires after 7 days idle. *Aggregate.* The committed purchase. Fields: id, customer id, status, totals, placed at. States: placed → paid → fulfilled → closed; cancelled from placed or paid. *Entity.* One SKU in an order. Fields: sku, quantity, unit price at purchase. **Rule:** price is copied, never re-read. *Value.* A promotion applied to an order. Fields: code, amount or percent. **Rule:** at most one per order. Money in and out. *Aggregate.* An attempt to collect money. Fields: id, order id, method, amount, status (authorised | captured | failed | refunded). *Entity.* Legal document issued when an order is paid. Fields: number, order id, billing address, tax lines. **Rule:** numbers are sequential per country. *Entity.* Money returned. Fields: payment id, amount, reason. **Rule:** total refunds ≤ captured amount. *Value.* Rate by country and product class. Fields: country, class, percent. Getting it to the customer. *Entity.* A stocking location. Fields: id, address, cut-off time. *Aggregate.* Goods leaving one warehouse for one order. Fields: id, order id, warehouse id, status (pending | packed | shipped | delivered). *Entity.* A physical box in a shipment. Fields: tracking number, carrier, weight. *Entity.* Goods coming back. Fields: order line, reason, received at.
