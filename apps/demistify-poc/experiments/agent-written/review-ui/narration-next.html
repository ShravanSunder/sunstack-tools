Evidence overview

Identity

The durable Session identity belongs to the running work. Replacing a connection must preserve that identity.

Connection

A Transport carries messages for one connection. Its closure does not establish whether the original Session survived.

Lookup

The Registry provides a stable lookup path. A successful reply alone does not prove that lookup returned the original work.

Expiry

Explicit expiry closes the recovery window. Missing and expired outcomes remain separately observable.

Admission

The caller must be admitted to the addressed Session. Attachment does not establish tenant isolation by itself.

Observation

Record the durable identity before disconnect and compare it after reconnect through the real target.

Workload

Keep the original workload active across the interruption. A replacement process with the same name is insufficient.

Evidence boundary

Source changes demonstrate the proposed behavior. Runtime observations establish what the running target actually did.

Narration evidence

The walkthrough carries its Markdown, subject icon and source-relative link to this page.

Review notes

Preserve the observed identity and the remaining proof gaps for review.
