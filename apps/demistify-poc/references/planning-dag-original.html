What is ready — and what is still owed

Ready for your review

#127 and #128 are open draft PRs with source-level review. F6 v3 is independently accepted at SPEC stage: four checks complete, four findings closed.

Next implementation

F6’s two planner runs are not started. They would be a dependent layer above #128 under a bounded commission. Acceptance of this spec alone does not dispatch work.

Separate lanes

Global/client token routing, installed refresh, behavior evaluations, Jev tooling and future watchdog work are separate work. This DAG makes none of them an automatic requirement or authorization.

Trust still needs evidence

Source agreement is a step toward reliability. It does not show agents consistently load the guidance, keep the design intact, choose useful tests or verify the actual feature.

The lead must keep the meaning

A verifier can produce a real observation of the wrong thing. The Lead must compare the required outcome with the target and evidence actually observed. More roles cannot replace that judgment.

How planning handles smaller repairs and plan storage

A positively source-proven mechanics-only improvement can retain its smaller regression/proof path. Missing Test Design or Verification Design never qualifies a design-bearing change for that shortcut.

Written plans default to a durable home under the explicit owner or governing repository policy; plan-only is a delivery terminal, not a storage rule. The adopted default is docs/specs/<spec>/plans/. The no-file-backed-spec dated work folder remains a proposed Lead default the owner can change.
