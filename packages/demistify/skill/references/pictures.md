# Pictures

A picture, screenshot or generated image is plain HTML: `<figure><img src="…" alt="…"><figcaption>…</figcaption></figure>`. Clicking opens it in the side panel beside the text, fitted to the screen, with an expand button for the full view. Several figures in an `ex-grid` make a gallery.

`<ex-compare>` shows before and after: two children side by side, each named by `label` (default Before / After). With `slider`, two same-size images overlay with a divider the reader drags.

In picture, Mermaid and other visual inspection views, hold Cmd (or Ctrl) and scroll to zoom around the pointer; hold the modifier and drag to pan. Ordinary scrolling stays native. Zoom/reset buttons remain available; reset returns to the initial view.
