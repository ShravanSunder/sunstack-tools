# Pictures

A picture, screenshot or generated image is plain HTML: `<figure><img src="…" alt="…"><figcaption>…</figcaption></figure>`. Clicking enlarges it. Several figures in an `ex-grid` make a gallery.

`<ex-compare>` shows before and after: two children side by side, each named by `label` (default Before / After). With `slider`, two same-size images overlay with a divider the reader drags.
